How it works

Five steps, and only one of them is yours.

The model is allowed to be fast. It is not allowed to be the one who says yes.

ModelReadCopies values out of the certificate, batch record and deviation log, each with the line it came from.
CodeCheckRefuses any claim its cited line does not support, then applies the specification revision that governed the lot.
AgentInvestigateChooses what to read next: a deviation record, an older revision, a corrected document. It can read; it cannot decide.
ModelProposeHolds the lot at once, or proposes release. A second reader checks the proposal against the findings.
YouDecideApprove, refuse, reject or overrule, with your name. A corrected document voids the decision and keeps it on record.

All documents, people, products and companies are invented. Built on Continuum, an open-source agent framework. Source on GitHub.
